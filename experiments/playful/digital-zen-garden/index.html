🧘 Digital Zen Garden

Create peaceful patterns in virtual sand. Inspired by Japanese rock gardens, this meditative experience lets you rake, draw, and place stones to craft your own moment of tranquility.

How to Interact:

  • Drag to rake patterns in the sand
  • Click to place stones and rocks
  • Use different tools for varied effects
  • Clear and start fresh anytime
  • Spacebar to reset garden

🍃 Take your time. There is no rush in the garden.

The garden teaches patience

About Digital Zen Garden

Inspired by Japanese Karesansui (dry landscape) gardens, this digital version captures the meditative practice of raking patterns in sand and arranging stones to create miniature landscapes.

Technical Details

Uses a pixel buffer to store "sand height" data. The rake tool creates ripple patterns by modifying neighboring pixels. Stones are rendered with radial gradients to create depth. The sand texture uses Perlin noise for organic variation.

Tools

  • Rake 🔱 - Create traditional raked patterns
  • Smooth 🌊 - Smooth and flatten areas
  • Stone 🪨 - Place decorative stones
  • Circle ⭕ - Draw circular patterns

Interaction Guide

  • Drag - Use current tool
  • Click - Place stone (in stone mode)
  • Space - Reset garden
  • S - Save your creation
  • 1-4 - Quick tool selection

The Philosophy

Zen gardens are not about the final result, but the process of creating. The impermanence of sand patterns teaches acceptance of change. Each rake stroke is a meditation.

Inspiration

Based on the rock gardens of Kyoto temples like Ryōan-ji and the meditative practices of Zen Buddhism. The digital medium makes this ancient practice accessible to all.
