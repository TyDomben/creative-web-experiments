About Theremin in Space

A digital recreation of the theremin, one of the earliest electronic musical instruments. The original theremin was controlled by hand proximity to two antennas - this version uses mouse position instead.

Technical Details

Built with the Web Audio API using OscillatorNode and GainNode. The frequency range is 100Hz to 2000Hz (roughly D2 to C7). Volume is controlled by Y-axis position with smooth ramping to prevent clicks.

The visualization uses FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) data from an AnalyserNode to create reactive graphics. Circles pulse with amplitude, colors shift with frequency.

How to Play

  • Mouse X - Pitch (left = low, right = high)
  • Mouse Y - Volume (top = quiet, bottom = loud)
  • 1-4 Keys - Change waveform
  • Space - Mute/Unmute

Waveforms

Sine: Pure, smooth tone
Square: Hollow, clarinet-like
Sawtooth: Bright, brassy
Triangle: Mellow, flute-like

Musical Notes

The display shows the nearest musical note to the current frequency. Try to "play" a melody by finding the right positions!

Inspiration

Created by Léon Theremin in 1920, the theremin was used in sci-fi movie soundtracks and by artists like The Beach Boys. This digital version brings that otherworldly sound to your browser.
