About Double Pendulum Chaos

The double pendulum is a classic example of a chaotic system. Despite being governed by deterministic physics equations, its behavior is practically unpredictable due to extreme sensitivity to initial conditions.

Technical Details

Simulates double pendulum motion using the Lagrangian mechanics equations. The system has two degrees of freedom (two angles) and conserves energy in the absence of friction.

Chaos Theory

The "butterfly effect" is clearly visible: launch two pendulums with nearly identical starting angles, and they will diverge into completely different motions within seconds.
